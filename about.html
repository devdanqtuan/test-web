About Arcix

Empowering Architectural Vision

We automate technical drafting and documentation directly inside SketchUp so architects, interior designers, and BIM managers can focus on pure design creativity.

Bridging the Gap Between Concept & Production

Architectural design should be about spatial experimentation, material tactile qualities, and sustainable innovation. Yet, up to 70% of an architect's working hours are consumed by repetitive 2D CAD drafting, manual hatching, layer alignments, and rebuilding 3D models into flat documentation sets.

Arcix was founded to eliminate this painful bottleneck. By embedding powerful parametric automation and BIM intelligence natively inside SketchUp, Arcix converts 3D models into clean, production-ready construction drawings, native AutoCAD DWG layers, and IFC4 schema models in seconds.
